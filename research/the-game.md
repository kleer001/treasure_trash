# The game this was gathered for

*Treasure Trash* — the block-pusher in the directory above this one. The other files here are
about other people's games; this one says who is reading them and why, so a finding like "the
crossing is unoccupied" has somebody standing in it.

**Status stamp: 2026-08-19.** Everything below is either what the tree held on that date or
what is INTENDED, and the two are marked apart. Nothing here is a commitment, a spec or a rule —
what the pieces do is `src/rules.js`, what a room is is `levels/`, and the pitch is
[`GAME-SHEET.md`](../GAME-SHEET.md). An intention written down is still an intention.

## What exists

It runs. `./run.sh` and a browser, or the Pages deploy — no install, no account, no launcher, and
the same page on a phone. The engine, the solver, the level format, the verifier and the
discovery pipeline are built and green, and there were **61 rooms across two acts** on the date
stamped above.

That matters against `marketing-playbooks.md` more than it looks. The Baba playbook's
load-bearing asset was *a free thing anyone could reach, two years before release* — and a game
that is already a URL does not have to build one. The artifact exists; what it has not had is
anybody pointed at it.

## What it is meant to be

Intent, not schedule. Every line here is the current plan and every one of them can move.

- **Web first, and free to play in the browser.** The demo is not a cut-down build to be made
  later; it is the game running on a page, which is what it has been the whole time.
- **A price on the full thing.** Somewhere in the field `competitive-landscape.md` measures —
  that shelf runs $4.99 to $29.99 and the reviews are Very or Overwhelmingly Positive across all
  of it, so the number is a question about what the rooms turn out to be worth, and it gets
  answered when they exist.
- **About a hundred rooms.** Two acts are down and the pipeline that finds them is running;
  `tools/` is most of the way to being a room factory with a person choosing at the end of it.
- **The pieces it already has.** The roster in `FORMATS.md`'s glyph table — bags, cans, bins,
  jugs, furniture, carts and boards, and the terrain lanes under them — is expected to be the
  roster it ships with. The remaining design work is what to build out of them, not what to add.
- **A level editor, maybe.** Baba shipped one and it is a live part of that game seven years on.
  Nothing here needs it and nothing here rules it out.

## What the research says it starts with

Stated as encouragement, and every one of them is an inference from the files beside this one.

- **The costume is proven and unattended.** `trash-panda-dossier.md` shows the raccoon fantasy
  pulling national press twice for a first-time solo developer with no industry contacts. The
  incumbent has been silent since June 2024. Nothing in the 2,297-deep itch sokoban field is
  wearing it.
- **The thing that game was missing is the thing this one is made of.** "Nothing to *do*" is the
  unanimous complaint in `review-teardown.md`, and a room with a par is an answer to it.
- **It is explainable in one clip.** `marketing-playbooks.md` finds that the most-watched video
  about a puzzle game is somebody else's essay about it. A mechanic you can show going wrong in
  five seconds is what recruits that person.
- **There is no launch window to miss.** Game Grumps found *Stephen's Sausage Roll* seven years
  in. A puzzle game that is good is never late, which is a real licence to take the time.

## What it still has to earn

The same files are just as clear about this, and it is not discouraging, it is the actual job.

- **The rooms.** Everything on that shelf is there because its levels are good, and no amount of
  positioning moves that number. This is the work, and it is the work that is currently underway.
- **Discovery.** A free browser puzzle game is invisible by default. The channels are named in
  `marketing-playbooks.md`, they are all open doors, and none of them opens by itself.
- **Presence.** Absence compounds exactly as hard as presence does — eighteen unanswered threads
  is what it looks like. Still being there in three years is a decision made repeatedly, cheaply,
  starting whenever.

## What it is not

Not shipped, not announced, not wishlisted, not dated. Nobody outside has played it. That is the
happy version of the position rather than the sad one: every finding in this directory arrived
early enough to be free.
