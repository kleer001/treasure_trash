## What stands out

Two of these are trivially cheap and were never answered:

- **"Add chirp."** A raccoon that makes a sound when it acts. Four people upvoted a four-word
  review asking for it — the cheapest possible request, never granted.
- **"Objectives/goals."** Somebody paid $10 and asked, on the storefront, for a raccoon game
  with a defined goal and a reason to prefer one action over another.

The rest are 3D-open-world asks (fast travel, a world map, traffic) that a one-screen grid does
not have the problem of.

## Before anyone answers one of these

Answering a lone voice is done on its merits and verified first, and posting publicly is a
decision for whoever owns the account. Nothing in this file has been posted anywhere. If any of
it ever is, it gets answered completely and honestly whether or not it promotes anything — a
self-promotional drive-by on a dead board is worse than silence.

Worth noting before anyone drafts one: several of these threads are on the storefront of a game
a person spent three years on and then went quiet about. There is a real human at the other end
of that silence, and a competitor's link is not what those threads are asking for.
