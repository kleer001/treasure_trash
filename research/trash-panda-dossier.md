## What it actually is

3D third-person free-roam. You are a raccoon on a Toronto street built from OpenStreetMap
data — real streets, real mailboxes and bus stops, named real neighbourhoods (Cabbagetown,
The Beach(es), Algonquin Island, Swansea, Morningside Heights). You knock over bins, eat what
spills, score points for mess. There are power-ups with cosmetic/movement effects, a garbage
truck on a fixed patrol route you can beat to the bins, and "nemesis" raccoons you can
challenge to a bin-knocking race.

No fail state, no timer, no level structure, no story, no puzzle.

## Press

The fantasy carried the coverage; the genre never entered into it. Every item is a
local-interest story about Toronto and raccoons, not a games-press story about a game.

- blogTO, May 2021 (prototype) and Nov 2023 (launch)
- CBC — video segment, "Become a garbage-picking raccoon in this new — and extremely Toronto — game"
- CTV News Toronto
- Daily Hive Toronto — two pieces (Business & Tech, Urbanized)
- Exclaim!, MobileSyrup, curiocity, mxdwn Games
- CFRB radio interview (referenced by a player on the Steam board)
- Kotaku carries a database entry only — no article

**Observation (this research, not a finding of fact):** the press hook was *place* plus
*animal*. Toronto's relationship with raccoons is a running local joke, and the game was the
joke made playable. That is a hook tied to a specific city, and it is not transferable to
anything set nowhere in particular — a game without a real place has to earn its hook from the
mechanic instead.

## Developer posture

Solo. Warm, present and chatty through launch; the announcements read like a person, not a
studio. Then silent from June 2024 onward, with no farewell post. Fifteen visible discussion
threads, **zero developer replies** on any of them, including direct feature requests and
"is anyone home".

The practical read: the raccoon-and-garbage niche on Steam has an incumbent who is not
defending it, whose customers are on record about what they wanted, and who never came back to
give it to them.
