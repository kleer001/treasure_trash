Treasure Trash — Spike

Throwaway feasibility spike · L0–L3 · raccoon only · does "fire your mess away from your path and your way out" feel good?

Move / strike / push: ↑↓←→ or WASD. U undo · R restart.
Goal: open every bag (pounce it), then stand on the exit. A bag bursts a 2×3 fan in the direction you strike; that trash stays.
Aiming (L1 and L2 only — the rooms that introduce a piece): press a direction once to aim and nothing happens yet; press it again to commit. Elsewhere one press acts, block-pusher style. Where a room does aim, it aims shoves as well as bursts — there's no pull, so a shove is no gentler than a burst. Walking is always one press.
The exit: plain floor — walk over it any time; it only counts once the last bag is torn (the sign lights up when it's live). Nothing may ever land on it — trash, cans and bags are all refused, so you can't bury your way out.
Blocked: if the rules refuse an action, the one cell doing the blocking flashes red with a ✕, you get a buzz, and the reason shows up top. Refused actions cost nothing.
Cans: push a full can → it slides one and ejects its bag one further; the empty can is then a normal pushable block. A can in the fan blocks a tear — relocate it first.
Trashed yourself into a corner? That's the puzzle — U / R.

Deterministic, no RNG. Not part of src/ — a spike to feel the mechanic before the design gate.
