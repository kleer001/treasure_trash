Move / strike / push: ↑↓←→ or WASD. U undo · R restart.
Goal: open every bag (pounce it), then stand on the exit. A bag bursts a 2×3 fan in the direction you strike; that trash stays.
The exit: plain floor — walk over it any time; it only counts once the last bag is torn (the sign lights up when it's live). It is not protected: trash or a pushed can lands on it and seals it for good.
Fan preview: stand next to a bag — the tiles that'll become trash glow yellow (valid) or red (blocked, can't strike that way).
Cans: push a full can → it slides one and ejects its bag one further; the empty can is then a normal pushable block.
Trashed yourself into a corner? That's the puzzle — U / R.

Deterministic, no RNG. Not part of src/ — a spike to feel the mechanic before the design gate.
