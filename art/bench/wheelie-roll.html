60 Hz, drawn on twos. The roll is the game's own: seven frames a cell on rollEase, stopped dead by the wall. Then the bin tips forward and rocks back. The bag lifts in the bin as the bin tips, and rises on as it rocks back. The rock back throws it: from the launch key it flies on its own curve, at the speed the bin gave it, into the cell the bin just left, and lands as the regular bag. The throw scales that launch speed; the arc is how high it comes down from.
