## Channels

Pick the few where this genre's players actually gather — name them specifically.
Don't spray every platform.

- **itch.io** — the store page is home base; enable devlogs.
- **Genre communities** — the specific subreddits, Discords, and forums for GENRE.
  (List the real ones here. Read each community's self-promotion rules first.)
- **Fediverse / Bluesky** — `#gamedev`, `#screenshotsaturday`, genre tags; post the GIF.
- **Short video** — a 10–20s loop clip where the hook is legible in the first 3s.
- **Curators / streamers** — a shortlist who cover this genre; a short honest pitch.

## Launch beats

- **Pre-launch:** itch page up as "coming soon," follow/wishlist ask; post the GIF
  and one devlog on the core mechanic; line up the community posts.
- **Launch day:** page live and playable; post to the listed communities (respecting
  each one's rules); pin the clip; reply to early comments fast.
- **Post-launch:** a devlog on what shipped and what's next; fold player feedback
  into the next MVP; note which channel actually brought players.

## Honesty guardrails (the release gate enforces these)

- **Accuracy:** every factual and historical claim on every surface is true and
  sourced — page, plan, clips, captions.
- **No overselling:** no superlatives the game can't earn; the pitch matches what the game
  delivers, so reviews aren't a correction.
- **Genre fit:** the copy speaks to the genre without pandering or promising depth
  that isn't there.

## What to watch

Page views → play rate → completion; which channel referred players; what the
first comments and reviews consistently say. Feed it back into the next game.
